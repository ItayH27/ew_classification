NBR  = (NIR − SWIR2) / (NIR + SWIR2)

Measurements

Specification

Imagery — Sentinel-2 surface reflectance, cloud-masked, sampled over a 20 km box at 78 m per pixel, 256 × 256 per scene.

Segmentation — 56.6M-parameter U-Net over six reflectance bands. IoU 0.803 on held-out validation; the 324M-parameter Prithvi-EO-2.0 baseline scores 0.813 on the same chips.

Severity — graded 1 minimal/none, 2 moderate, 3 maximal from NDVI and NBR statistics inside the mask. Post-event imagery only.

Attribution — weighs media evidence alone. It is not a forensic or legal finding.
